\item (Jan 12) a)
Let $A$ be a commutative ring and $M$ an $A$-module. $M$ is Noetherian if
any submodule of $M$ is finitely generated or equivalently if it satisfies 
the ascending chain condition. 
Let $0\rightarrow M'\rightarrow M\rightarrow M
''\rightarrow 0$ be a short exact sequence. Show that $M$ is Noetherian if
and only if $M',M''$ are Noetherian. %[AM] pg 115

b) Let $u:M\rightarrow M$ be a surjective homomorphism of $A$-modules.
If $M$ is Noetherian, show that $u$ is an isomorphism.
(Hint: consider $Ker u^m$ for all $m>0$.) 
Give an example of a non Noetherian $A$-module $M$ where this fails.

c) If $A$ is a commutative ring and $f=a_nx^n+....+a_0$ is a zero divisor 
in $A[x],$ then there is an element $0\ne a\in A$ such that
$aa_n=aa_{n-1}=...=aa_0=0$. (Hint: Pick $g=b_0+...+b_mx^m$ of smallest degree 
such that $fg=0$. Then as $a_nb_m=0$, one sees that $a_ng=0$ (as it has 
smaller degree and $a_ngf=0$). Show that $a_{n-r}g=0$ for $0\leq r\leq n$. )

d) Show that $x^2+3x+2$ is irreducible in $\mathbb{Z}[[x]]$ but not in
$\mathbb{Z}[x]$.

e) Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $f=a_nx^n+....+a_1x+a_0\in A[x]$.
Then $f$ is a unit if and only if $a_0$ is a unit and $a_1,...,a_n$ are 
nilpotent (i.e. $a_i^{n_i}=0$ some $n_i>0$).
(Hint: you will need to show by induction that $a_{n}^{r+1}b_{m-r}=0$
for $0\leq r\leq m$ where $g=b_0+...+b_mx^m$ and $fg=1$.)
